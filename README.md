#  Sleepy dreamy memories

You explore dreams and memories where your choices decide which side of the story you live.

# Pitch 

- My project is a narrative role-playing video game where players travel into colourful, cartoon-like dreams to explore memories and meet other people and creatures.
- Inside one of these dreams, the player faces a bullying situation and makes choices that determine the story.
- The music and visual mood change according to the characters' emotions.
- Every choice shapes the story and gives players the chance to understand bullying from different sides.
